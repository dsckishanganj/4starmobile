7-Point Quality Inspection Standard

Chahe hum display combo receive karein, battery ka batch check karein ya high-wattage fast charger—hamara 7-stage quality checklist follow hota hai:

Stage 01

1. 10-Point Multi-Touch Grid Test

Screen ke har kone par drag, pinch-to-zoom aur fast typing check ki jaati hai taaki koi dead zone ya ghost touch na ho.

Stage 02

2. Battery Internal Resistance (IR) Check

BMS circuit aur battery core ka mili-ohm resistance naapa jaata hai taaki load padne par battery sudden restart na ho.

Stage 03

3. GaN Charger Thermal & Ripple Test

65W aur 80W turbo chargers ko continuous 30-minute load par chalakar thermal imaging aur voltage stability dekhi jaati hai.

Stage 04

4. Braided Cable Tension & 6A Current Test

Type-C cables ke neck joint par bend test aur actual 6A super-fast current throughput meter se pass karwaya jaata hai.

Stage 05

5. Tempered Glass 9H Scratch & Impact Test

Steel ball drop test aur key-scratch resistance check karke real 11D/9D full glue coating ki durability confirm hoti hai.

Stage 06

6. CC Board Charging Port & Mic Continuity

Sub-board ke fast charging pins, digital mic frequency response aur RF network line grounding ka point-to-point test.
