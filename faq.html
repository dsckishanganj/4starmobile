Q1. Kya aapke paas sabhi mobile models ke display aur folders milte hain?
Jee haan, hamare paas sabhi leading brands (Samsung, Vivo, Oppo, Realme, Xiaomi, OnePlus) ke original quality LCD display, OG folders aur glass testing facility uplabdh hain.
Q2. Battery replacement par kya warranty rehti hai?
Mobile battery replacements par 6 mahine ki standard backup warranty di jaati hai, jisme backup na milne par replacement support milta hai.

Hamare paas 33W, 65W, 80W, 120W Super Fast Type-C chargers, braided data cables aur certified adapters stock mein rehte hain.
