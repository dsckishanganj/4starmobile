Store Rules & Warranty Terms

Hamara maksad aapko behtareen quality aur transparent service dena hai.

1. Display & Folder Testing Policy:
Folder lagane se pehle customer shop par poori tarah touch aur display check kar sakte hain. Fitting (glue lagne) aur screen patti katne ke baad replacement valid nahi hota.
2. Battery Replacement:
Battery backup testing warranty 6 mahine ki hoti hai. Phooli hui (puffed) ya physical damage battery warranty mein cover nahi hoti.
3. Accessories & Chargers:
Fast chargers par manufacturing defect testing policy lagu rehti hai.
