Mobile Spare Parts, Tools & Machinery: Complete Technical Encyclopedia

Mobile repairing aur genuine spare parts ka kaam sirf saman bechne tak simit nahi hai—yeh ek high-precision micro-engineering field hai. Ek modern smartphone ke andar hazaaron microscopic components, multi-layer printed circuit boards (PCB), sensitive power ICs aur ultrafine display ribbon flex hote hain. Chahe aap ek professional technician hon, ek dukan chalate hon, ya fir apne phone ke hardware ke bare mein janna chahte hon—4 STAR MOBILE Kishanganj ka yeh detailed technical article aapko har ek tool, part aur unki pehchan ki poori jankari pradan karega.

1. Mobile Repair Lab Ki Zaroori Machinery & Tools

Smartphone ke nano-components ko replace karne ke liye sadharan tools kaam nahi aate. Lab mein advance calibrated machines ka hona zaroori hota hai:

1. SMD Rework Station (Hot Air Gun)

Upyog: Motherboard par chips, charging ports, SIM trays aur micro mic ko desolder aur resolder karne ke liye accurate hot air flow deliver karta hai.

Safe Temp: 350°C se 380°C par airflow control ke sath PCB track safe rehte hain.

2. Precision Soldering Iron & Jumper

Upyog: Broken PCB traces ko join karne aur CC board ke charging legs ko mazboot pakad dene ke liye ultra-fine conical tip iron ka prayog hota hai.

Jumper Wire: 0.02mm insulated copper wire se microscopic track bypass kiye jaate hain.

3. Regulated DC Power Supply

Upyog: Short-circuit detection aur boot sequence testing. Milliampere (mA) reading se pata chalta hai ki CPU dead hai ya display supply cut hai.

Feature: Overcurrent protection buzzer ke sath short killer facility.

4. LCD Vacuum Separator Machine

Upyog: Tute hue glass ko touch combo se alag karne ke liye 80°C constant heating plate aur strong built-in vacuum suction pump.

Result: Original LCD bacha rehta hai aur sirf upar ka front glass change ho jaata hai.

2. Smartphone Motherboard (PCB) Ke Mukhya Spare Parts

Motherboard par lage har ek component ka apna critical function hota hai. Yahan unke baare mein jankari di gayi hai:

Micro Circuitry: Power IC, CPU, Flash Storage (eMMC/UFS) aur RF Network Section.
  • Sub-Board / Charging CC Board: Phone ke bottom section mein hone wala yeh board Type-C jack, analog mic, vibration motor aur cellular antenna line ko carry karta hai. Original CC boards mein surge protection diodes aur quick-charge resistors lage hote hain jo fast charge ko bina overheat ke support karte hain.
  • Power Management IC (PMIC): Phone ka dil aur dimaag. Battery ke raw voltage (3.8V-4.4V) ko convert karke processor, camera aur RAM ke liye alag-alag micro-voltages (0.8V, 1.2V, 1.8V, 2.8V) produce karta hai.
  • Network WTR / PA IC: Radio frequency receiver aur Power Amplifier (PA) chip jo weak network areas mein bhi stable 4G/5G signal catch karne mein madad karti hai.
  • Audio Codec IC: Phone call ke dauran ear-speaker, loud speaker aur noise cancellation mic ke audio signals ko decode karta hai.

3. LCD Display Combos: Quality, Types aur Testing Counter

Screen replacement mobile repair ka sabse aam hissa hai, lekin yahan quality ka farq sabse bada hota hai:

Testing Counter Facility: Dukan par display fit hone se pehle live brightness, refresh rate aur touch check karne ki suvidha.
  1. Original Service Pack Grade: 100% natural color calibration, factory brightness (up to 1200+ nits), 90Hz/120Hz smooth scrolling aur accurate multi-touch support.
  2. INCELL / OLED Premium Grade: Market ka sabse popular replacement combo jisme thin glass profile aur lag-free touch milta hai. Sahi fitting ke baad phone factory condition jaisa lagta hai.
  3. Fitting Rules & Glue Protection: Display fitting ke liye hum recommended T-7000 / B-7000 adhesive use karne ki salah dete hain. Gum ko frame par barabar lagakar rubber-band tensioning 2-3 ghante rakhne se screen par light leakage ya touch ghosting ki samasya nahi aati.

4. Genuine Replacement Batteries & BMS Board Security

Sasti duplicate battery lagane se backup nahi milta aur phone restart ya overheat hone lagta hai. Genuine batteries mein yeh khasiyat hoti hain:

  • Original BMS Circuit: Battery Management System board jo overcharging, deep discharge aur short circuit par power supply turant cut-off kar deta hai.
  • High Cycle Life: Grade-A lithium cells jo 600 se 800 charge cycles ke baad bhi 85%+ health maintain rakhte hain.
  • Zero Swelling Guarantee: Heat dissipation layer phone ko heavy gaming aur turbo charging ke dauran thanda rakhti hai.
