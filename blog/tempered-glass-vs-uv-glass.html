Normal Glass, 11D Full Glue Ya UV Glass? Apne Phone Ke Liye Sahi Protector Kaise Chunein

By 4 STAR MOBILE • Gandhi Chowk & Mahavir Marg, Kishanganj

Market mein 50 rupaye se lekar 500 rupaye tak ke screen guard milte hain. Sahi glass na lagwane par ya toh touch slow ho jata hai ya fir girte hi display andar se toot jata hai.

1. 11D / 9D Full Glue Glass (Flat Screen Ke Liye Best)

Yeh pure screen par glue ke saath chipakta hai. Kinaron par black border hone ki wajah se bubble nahi banta aur cover lagane par glass ukharata nahi hai.

2. Curved Display Ke Liye UV Liquid Glass

Special Tip: Agar aapka phone curved screen (jaise OnePlus, Realme Pro, Vivo V series) hai, toh normal guard side se ukhad jayega. Iske liye UV glue aur UV light machine se fit kiya gaya glass hi 100% fitting deta hai.

3. Matte vs Privacy Glass

• Matte / Gaming Glass: Fingerprint nahi chhodta aur smooth scrolling deta hai.
• Privacy Glass: Side se dekhne wale ko screen bilkul black dikhti hai, bus aur train mein privacy ke liye best hai.
