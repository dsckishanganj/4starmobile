Emergency Mobile Care

Mobile Paani Mein Gir Jaye Toh Turant Kya Karein? Yeh Galti Kabhi Mat Karna

By 4 STAR MOBILE • 20+ Years Trusted Store • Kishanganj

Barsaat ke mausam mein ya galti se phone paani ya balti mein gir jana ek emergency situation hoti hai. Zyadatar log aisi galatiyan kar baithte hain jisse theek hone wala phone bhi hamesha ke liye dead ho jata hai.

1. Sabse Badi Galti: Charger Lagana Ya On Rakhna

Strict Rule: Paani ghusne ke baad phone ko check karne ke liye charger mein kabhi na lagayein! Current daudte hi motherboard ki main power IC short ho jati hai. Phone ko turant switch off kar dein.

2. Chawal (Rice) Mein Daalna: Fayda Ya Nuksan?

Chawal bahar ka paani thoda sokhta hai lekin motherboard ke andar moisture bana rehta hai, jisse 2-3 din mein corrosion (jung) lag jata hai. Chawal ka barik powder charging port aur headphone jack ko aur block kar deta hai.

3. Sahi Tarika: Ultrasonic Cleaning & Chemical Wash

Phone ko jald se jald kisi technician ke paas le jayein jahan battery disconnect karke board ko Iso-Propyl Alcohol (IPA) se wash kiya jata hai aur SMD blower se dry kiya jata hai.
