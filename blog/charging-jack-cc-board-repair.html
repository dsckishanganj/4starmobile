Data Cable Hilane Par Charge Hota Hai? CC Board Aur Charging Pin Ki Jankari

By 4 STAR MOBILE • Fal Chowk & Mahavir Marg, Kishanganj

Aksar dekha jata hai ki phone mein charger lagane ke baad cable ko kisi angle par modna ya hilana padta hai tab jaakar charging show hoti hai. Kuch log ise ignore karte hain jab tak phone poori tarah band na ho jaye.

1. Dust (Dhool-Mitti) Jama Hona

Pocket ki gandagi aur rui (lint) charging port ke andar jama ho jati hai, jisse type-C pin poori tarah andar fit nahi baithti. Isko kisi safety needle ya brush se dheere se saaf karne par problem solve ho sakti hai.

2. Pin Loose Hona vs CC Board Replacement

Samjhein farq: Agar sirf pin dheeli hai toh soldering iron se pin badalna kaafi hota hai. Lekin agar network IC, mic ya fast charge line damage ho, toh poora Sub-Board (CC Board) change karna hi sabse durable solution hota hai.

3. Fast Charging Disconnect Hone Ki Wajah

Duplicate ya loose charging board lagwane se mic ki aawaz dheemi ho jati hai aur fast charging ka icon nahi aata. Hamesha original IC wala CC board hi lagwayein.

Charging Problem Check Karwana Hai?

Original Type-C connector aur branded CC boards turant fitting ke liye uplabdh hain.
