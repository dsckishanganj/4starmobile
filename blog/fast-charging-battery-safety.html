Battery & Charger Guide

Kya Fast Charging Se Phone Ki Battery Jaldi Kharab Hoti Hai? Jaaniye Sahi Sach

By 4 STAR MOBILE • Gandhi Chowk, Kishanganj

Aaj kal har smartphone ke saath 33W, 67W, 80W ya 120W ka fast charger mil raha hai. Lekin bohot se grahak puchte hain: "Bhaiya, tezi se charge karne par phone garam hota hai, kya battery fool jayegi?"

1. Fast Charging Ka Asal Kaam Karne Ka Tarika

Fast chargers 2 phases mein kaam karte hain: 0% se 70-80% tak full power mein tezi se current dete hain, aur 80% ke baad current ko dheema kar dete hain taaki battery heat na ho. Is process ko trickle charging kaha jata hai.

2. Asli Dushman: Heat (Garmi) Aur Sasta Local Adapter

Khatra: Saste duplicate chargers mein voltage regulator circuit nahi hota. Wo constant high voltage bhejte rehte hain jisse battery ka chemical dry hone lagta hai aur battery fool jati hai.

3. Battery Ki Zindagi Lambi Rakhne Ke 3 Aasan Niyam

• Phone ko 20% se neeche aane se pehle charge par lagayein aur 85-90% tak charge karein.
• Charging ke waqt bhari gaming ya video recording karne se bachein.
• Motay cover ko charge karte waqt nikaal dein taaki heat aasani se bahar nikal sake.
