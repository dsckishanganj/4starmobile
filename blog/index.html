📅 15 October 2026 ⚡ Battery Care Tip

💡 Phone Ki Battery Jaldi Khatam Ho Rahi Hai? Ye 1 Aadat Badlein!

Har waqt mobile internet (Data) ON rakhne se background apps continuous refresh hote rehte hain, jisse processor garam hota hai aur battery health tezi se drop hoti hai.

Kamyabi ke 2 Tips:
1. Jab zaroorat na ho, mobile data OFF rakhein.
2. Raat ko sote waqt internet band zaroor karein — phone cool rahega aur battery cycle lambe samay tak safe rahegi.
